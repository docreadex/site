About DocReadex: PDF & Office Viewer

DocReadex: PDF & Office Viewer is a free Android app that lets you open, view, and manage documents of all types - plus a document scanner and powerful PDF tools to scan, create, merge, extract and convert PDFs right on your phone.

Most document viewer apps charge for basic features or show constant ads. DocReadex was built to give everyone a clean, fast, and completely free experience.

DocReadex is a brand name of Ghanshyam Dhanore, an individual developer.

Document Scanner

Turn your phone's camera into a document scanner. Everything happens on your phone - nothing is uploaded.

📷 Take a Picture

Photograph a page with the built-in camera (flash and tap-to-focus included), or choose an existing photo from your gallery.

✂️ Auto-Detect Page Edges

DocReadex finds the edges of the page and straightens it. Drag the corners to fine-tune, or keep the whole photo.

🎨 Clean Up the Scan

Choose Original, Enhanced, Gray or Black & White, adjust brightness and contrast, and rotate left or right.

📄 Scan to PDF

In PDF Tools, tap Take a Photo to scan a page straight into your PDF. Scan as many pages as you need, then create the PDF. Or save a single scan as a picture on your phone.

PDF Tools

Built-in PDF tools - no separate app needed. PDFs you create are saved in the Download/DocReadex folder on your phone.

🖼 Images to PDF

Convert photos from your gallery or camera into a PDF. Choose page size (A4, A3, Letter), portrait or landscape, and fit or fill. Drag to reorder pages, and crop or clean up each picture before you save.

🔗 Merge PDFs

Combine two or more PDF files into a single document. Pick PDFs from your device, put them in the order you want, give the file a name, and merge with one tap.

✂️ Extract PDF Pages

Open a PDF and see every page as a thumbnail. Tap the pages you want, use Select all, or type a range like 1-3, 5, 8-10. Save them as a new PDF.

📑 Convert to PDF

Turn Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV, text, JSON and Markdown files into PDF. Open the file, choose Convert to PDF in the menu, then save or share it with Android's Save as PDF.

Powerful Viewers

Each file type opens in a viewer made for it.

{ } JSON, YAML, Markdown, Text

🖼 Images

Open JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP pictures with pinch-to-zoom and pan.
