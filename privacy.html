Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DocReadex: PDF & Office Viewer ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information. DocReadex is a brand name of Ghanshyam Dhanore, an individual developer (not a registered company). We take your privacy seriously and have designed this application to operate entirely on-device.

1. Information We Collect

DocReadex does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any identifiers.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services that send data externally.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or third-party tracking.
  • Files you open, scan, or convert (documents, images, PDFs) are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

2. Permissions Used

The app asks for one device permission, and only to provide its core functionality:

CameraUsed only when you tap "Take a Photo" in the document scanner or the Images to PDF tool to capture a picture. Photos are saved on your device only; nothing is sent to any server.

The app does not ask for any other permission. In particular:

Photos and gallery: no permissionWhen you choose a photo from your gallery in the document scanner or the Images to PDF tool, Android's own photo picker opens. The app receives only the photos you choose, processes them on-device, and never uploads or shares them.
Files and storage: no permission on Android 10 and newerDocuments (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, TXT, images) are opened through Android's file picker, which gives the app access only to the files you select. PDFs and pictures you create are saved to the DocReadex folder inside your device's Download folder. On Android 9 and older, the app asks for the legacy storage write permission, used only to save those files. No files are accessed without your explicit action.

3. Local Data Storage

The only data saved by the app on your device is:

  • A list of recently opened file paths, stored in the app's local SharedPreferences solely to display your recent files list. This data never leaves your device.
  • PDF files you create are saved to the DocReadex folder inside your device's Download folder. You have full control over these files at all times.

4. Third-Party Services

DocReadex does not integrate any third-party services, APIs, cloud platforms, or SDKs that transmit data off your device. The app functions fully offline.

Nothing is uploaded by the app. A file leaves your device only when you tap Share and pick another app to receive it, and then that app's own privacy policy applies. The email and Instagram links on the About page open in other apps you already have.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. No personal data is collected at all.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the updated date at the top of this page. If a change is material, we will tell you in the app or on the app's store listing.
